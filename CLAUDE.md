# `CLAUDE.md — Section SEO + Conversion Upgrade System`

```md
# CLAUDE.md — Section SEO + Conversion Upgrade System

## OBJECTIVE

Upgrade every website section so it is not just visually clean, but also:

- SEO-supported
- conversion-focused
- locally relevant
- trust-building
- easy to scan
- connected to the next action

The goal is to make every section carry weight for both users and search engines.

---

## SYSTEM AWARENESS

This website is a lead generation system.

Every section must answer at least one of these:

- What does the company do?
- Where does the company serve?
- Why should someone trust them?
- What problem does this solve?
- What should the visitor do next?

A section that only looks good but says nothing is incomplete.

---

## PLAN MODE REQUIRED

Before editing any section, define:

- Section name
- Current purpose
- Current weakness
- SEO opportunity
- Conversion opportunity
- Final goal

Example:

Section:
Reviews

Weakness:
Strong visuals, but no service or location context

SEO opportunity:
Mention grading, paving, concrete, and South Bay naturally

Conversion opportunity:
Use reviews to reinforce trust before pushing estimate request

---

## SECTION STRUCTURE RULE

Every major section should include:

1. Eyebrow / label
2. Clear headline
3. Short subtext
4. SEO support paragraph
5. Main content
6. CTA or next-step bridge when appropriate

---

## SEO SUPPORT LAYER

Every major section should include one short SEO-support paragraph.

This paragraph must:

- be 1 to 2 sentences max
- mention service category naturally
- mention location naturally if relevant
- reinforce trust, process, or outcome
- avoid keyword stuffing

Good example:

Homeowners and businesses across the South Bay trust JC Grading for grading, paving, and concrete work that holds up over time. These reviews reflect the same things we focus on every job: proper prep, clear communication, and reliable results.

Bad example:

JC Grading is the best grading paving concrete South Bay company for grading paving concrete services in South Bay.

---

## COPY STYLE RULES

Copy must sound:

- direct
- human
- contractor-aware
- confident
- specific
- easy to understand

Avoid:

- fluff
- fake luxury language
- generic AI phrases
- long paragraphs
- vague claims
- keyword stuffing

Forbidden phrases:

- quality you can trust
- customer satisfaction is our priority
- we are passionate about excellence
- premium solutions
- tailored solutions
- unmatched service

---

## SECTION AUDIT METHOD

For every section, check:

### 1. Does it explain what this company does?

If no, add service context.

### 2. Does it explain where they work?

If no, add local context naturally.

### 3. Does it build trust?

If no, add proof, process, experience, reviews, or clear reasoning.

### 4. Does it move the visitor forward?

If no, add CTA or next-step bridge.

### 5. Does it feel human?

If no, rewrite with simpler, more direct language.

---

## SECTION TYPES AND RULES

## HERO SECTION

Must include:

- main service
- location
- clear positioning
- primary CTA
- secondary CTA

The hero must be understood in under 3 seconds.

Avoid vague headlines.

Good direction:
Graded right. Poured once.

Support with:
- South Bay
- grading
- paving
- concrete
- prep-first positioning

---

## SERVICES SECTION

Must explain:

- what services are offered
- why prep matters
- what outcome the customer gets

SEO support paragraph example:

JC Grading provides grading, paving, concrete, drainage, excavation, and repair services across the South Bay with a focus on proper site prep and long-term surface performance.

Each service card must include:

- specific service name
- simple explanation
- outcome or reason it matters

---

## PROJECTS SECTION

Must explain:

- real project types
- service relevance
- proof of work

SEO support paragraph example:

These projects show the type of grading, paving, concrete, and site prep work JC Grading handles for South Bay residential and commercial properties.

Each project should include:

- project type
- service category
- location if available
- short outcome-focused description

---

## REVIEWS SECTION

Must connect reviews to services and location.

SEO support paragraph example:

Homeowners and businesses across the South Bay trust JC Grading for grading, paving, and concrete work that holds up over time. These reviews reflect proper prep, clear communication, and reliable results.

Reviews should reinforce:

- communication
- schedule reliability
- quality work
- clean handoff
- trust

---

## PROCESS SECTION

The process section must not just explain steps.

It must explain why the process prevents bad outcomes.

The goal is to make the visitor feel:

- they know what they are doing
- they check the important details
- they do not skip prep
- the job will not fail because of shortcuts

---

## PROCESS STEP COPY

### STEP 01 — CALL OR FORM

Use:

Tell us where the project is, what surface you’re dealing with, and what you’re trying to fix or build.

We’ll ask the right questions upfront so we don’t waste your time with guesses or vague pricing.

---

### STEP 02 — SITE WALK

Use:

We look at access, grading, drainage, base conditions, and finish requirements before anything gets quoted.

Most issues come from what’s underneath. This is where we make sure it’s done right.

---

### STEP 03 — BUILD

Use:

We prep, install, and clean up with a clear scope and no shortcuts.

You get a finished surface that holds up and a direct line if anything comes up after.

---

## PROCESS REINFORCEMENT BLOCK

Add directly under the process cards:

Every project is built on proper prep, drainage, and base work. That’s why our surfaces last and why clients do not have to redo the job a year later.

Recommended CTA under this block:

Get My Free Estimate

Small support text:

Takes 30 seconds. Real response within 24 hours.

---

## ABOUT / WHY US SECTION

Must explain:

- experience
- prep-first philosophy
- what makes the company different
- why clients trust them

SEO support paragraph example:

With years of hands-on grading, paving, and concrete experience, JC Grading helps South Bay homeowners and businesses avoid surface failures caused by poor prep, drainage issues, and rushed work.

---

## CONTACT / FORM SECTION

Must include service and location context.

SEO support paragraph example:

Looking for grading, paving, concrete, drainage, or excavation work in the South Bay? Send the project details and JC Grading will follow up with a clear next step.

Contact section must include:

- simple form
- phone CTA
- response expectation
- no spam reassurance

---

## CTA RULES

Every page must include:

- CTA above the fold
- CTA after trust-building sections
- CTA near the final form

CTA examples:

- Get My Free Estimate
- Request Free Estimate
- Call Now
- Start My Estimate

CTA support text examples:

- Takes 30 seconds.
- Real response within 24 hours.
- Free estimates. Real answers.

---

## LOCAL SEO RULES

Use local terms naturally:

- South Bay
- homeowners
- businesses
- residential properties
- commercial properties

Do not force city names unless they are relevant.

Avoid repeating the same keyword phrase too often.

---

## INTERNAL LINKING RULES

Every section with services should connect to:

- Services page
- Contact page
- Estimate form

Every project section should connect to:

- Services
- Contact
- Similar project categories

Every CTA must point somewhere real.

No dead links.

No placeholder anchors.

---

## BUTTON AND LINK QA

Before completion, verify:

- Get My Free Estimate scrolls to the estimate form or opens the contact page
- Request Free Estimate goes to the correct form
- Call Now uses a valid tel link
- Contact links go to the correct contact section or page
- Service cards link correctly if clickable
- Project cards link correctly if clickable
- Social links work if included

Forbidden:

- href="#"
- empty links
- fake buttons
- clickable cards with no destination

---

## MOBILE RULES

Mobile must be treated as primary.

Check:

- section text does not feel too long
- SEO paragraphs are readable
- CTAs are easy to tap
- cards stack cleanly
- process steps are easy to scan
- no horizontal scrolling
- form is easy to complete

---

## FORM RULES

The estimate form must collect:

- name
- phone
- email
- address or city
- service needed
- project details

The form must:

- submit correctly
- not redirect unless intentionally designed
- show success confirmation
- show error state if submission fails
- reset after successful submission

---

## QA CHECKLIST

Before completion, verify every section:

### Content

- Section has a clear purpose
- SEO support line exists where useful
- Services are mentioned naturally
- Location is mentioned naturally
- Copy is specific and human
- No generic filler

### Conversion

- CTA exists where needed
- CTA path is clear
- Trust is reinforced
- Visitor knows next step

### SEO

- Main services are represented
- Local relevance is clear
- No keyword stuffing
- Headings support the page topic

### Technical

- Buttons work
- Links work
- Mobile layout works
- Form works
- No console errors

---

## SELF-IMPROVEMENT LOOP

After every issue, create a rule.

Example:

Issue:
Review section looks good but has no SEO context.

Rule:
Every review section must include one short paragraph tying reviews to service, location, and trust.

Example:

Issue:
Process section explains steps but does not build authority.

Rule:
Every process step must explain why that step prevents project failure or confusion.

---

## FINAL STANDARD

Do not ship until every major section:

- looks clean
- explains something useful
- supports SEO
- builds trust
- moves the visitor toward action
- works on mobile
- has no broken links or buttons

If a section only looks good but does not help ranking or conversion, rewrite it.
```
