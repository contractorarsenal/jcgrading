Sealcoating protects asphalt from water intrusion, UV damage, and fuel spills while refreshing how the surface looks. It works on asphalt in good structural condition. It is not a substitute for base repair.

Questions

Common questions

Is sealcoating the right fix for cracks?

Small surface cracks can be sealed first. If the base has failed, a sealcoat will not fix it, so we check the base before recommending a coat.

Should I patch before sealcoating?

Yes. Repairs go first, because sealcoating is for asphalt in good structural condition.

Do you stripe after sealcoating?

Yes. Striping is done after sealcoat work as part of the same project.
