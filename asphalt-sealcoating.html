Sealcoating for asphalt that is still structurally sound

Sealcoating protects good asphalt from water, UV damage, and fuel spills. It does not fix a failed base, so if your surface needs repair first, we will say so before we seal it.

JC Grading sealcoats driveways and parking lots across the South Bay and Peninsula, after checking that the surface is worth protecting.

How we do it

What happens before any sealer goes down

  1. 1. Condition checkWe check whether the base is still sound. Sealcoating is only for asphalt in good structural condition.
  2. 2. Crack sealingCracks are sealed first. Sealing them early stops water from getting under the base and accelerating failure.
  3. 3. SealcoatThe sealcoat goes on the sound surface.
  4. 4. Striping, if neededFresh striping for parking lots, fire lanes, and ADA stalls is done after sealcoating, as part of the same project.

When sealcoating is the wrong step

Widespread cracking, soft spots, or sections that have settled need repair first.

Sealer over a failed base hides the problem. It does not stop it.

Questions

Common questions

Does sealcoating fix cracks?

No. Crack sealing is a separate step. Sealing cracks early stops water getting under the base, and sealcoating does not replace it.

What surfaces do you sealcoat?

Driveways and parking lots in good structural condition. Failed sections need repair first.

Do you stripe after sealcoating?

Yes. Striping is done after paving or sealcoating as part of the same project.
