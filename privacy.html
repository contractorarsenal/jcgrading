Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

Who this policy covers

This policy describes how JC Grading Paving Solution, Inc. ("JC Grading," "we," "us") handles information collected through this website, jcgradingpavingsolution.com. It does not cover information collected offline, such as during a phone call or an in-person site visit.

Information you submit to us

When you fill out an estimate or contact form on this site, we ask for information needed to follow up on your project, which may include:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Project address or city
  • Service needed, project timeline, and budget range
  • Project description or details you provide
  • Photos you optionally choose to upload

This information is submitted through our form provider, Web3Forms, and delivered to JC Grading by email so our team can respond to your request. We use it only to follow up about the project you asked about — not for unrelated marketing, and not sold to third parties.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, this site automatically collects some technical information when you visit, including your device and browser type, approximate location derived from IP address, pages viewed, and how you interact with the page (clicks, scrolling, time on page). This is collected through the analytics tools described below.

Analytics tools we use

We use the following third-party analytics services to understand how visitors use this site and to improve it:

  • Google Analytics (GA4). Collects usage data such as pages visited, general location, device/browser type, and on-site interactions such as button clicks. We do not send names, emails, phone numbers, or project details to Google Analytics.
  • Microsoft Clarity. Records anonymized session behavior (clicks, scrolling, page navigation) to help us understand and improve the site experience. Clarity is configured to mask sensitive form input by default, and we do not manually send names, emails, phone numbers, or addresses to Clarity as custom data.

These providers may set cookies or use similar technologies in your browser. You can control cookies through your browser settings, and browser extensions or ad blockers may prevent some or all of this data collection.

Google Maps / address autocomplete

If enabled, the address field on our estimate forms uses Google's Places API to suggest address matches as you type, to save you from typing a full address manually. This sends what you type to Google to generate suggestions. You can always ignore the suggestions and type your address manually instead.

How we use your information

We use the information you submit to respond to your estimate request, schedule a site visit if applicable, and communicate with you about your project. We use aggregated, anonymized analytics data to understand site traffic and improve page content and performance.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share form submissions with our form-processing provider (Web3Forms) solely to deliver your message to us. We share anonymized usage data with our analytics providers (Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity) as described above. We may also disclose information if required by law.

Data retention

We retain estimate and contact form submissions for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your inquiry and maintain business records. You can request that we delete information you've submitted by contacting us using the details below.

Your choices

You can decline to submit a form and instead contact us by phone or email. You can block or clear cookies in your browser at any time. To ask us to delete information you've previously submitted, email us at the address below.

Children's privacy

This website is intended for adults seeking contracting services and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our tools or practices change. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your information can be sent to projects@jcgradingpavingsolution.com or by calling (408) 656-8639.

This page describes our data practices in plain terms and is not a substitute for legal advice. It does not represent a claim of compliance with any specific privacy law or certification.
