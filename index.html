Who We Are

Welcome to JC Grading Paving Solution

With over 20 years of hands-on experience, we bring precision, quality, and expertise to every project. Founded in April 2024, our mission is to deliver top-tier grading, paving, and concrete services across the Bay Area and beyond. From driveways and patios to parking lots and roads, we take pride in transforming spaces that last.

Behind the scenes, our dedicated operations team keeps projects moving — managing schedules, permits, logistics, and communication to ensure everything runs smoothly from start to finish. Every team member is trained in safety protocols, quality standards, and customer service, so our clients can expect professionalism at every stage of the job.

Our Story

Meet the Owner, Jose C.

I have worked in the construction industry for over 10 years, following in the footsteps of my father and other family members. I worked for another company for most of my time in the industry and I always had a dream to own my own company and build a successful company and a legacy I could pass down to my children. I decided that if I wanted to build a company like I had dreamt about, I needed to get started. I founded JC Grading Paving Solution, Inc. in 2024.
