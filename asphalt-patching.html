Asphalt patching that fixes the cause

A patch over a failed base comes back. We remove the deteriorated material down to a stable base, then rebuild it properly.

JC Grading patches potholes, cracks, and failed asphalt sections across the South Bay and Peninsula. The repair goes down to a stable base, not just over the surface.

What it covers

What we do

  • Pothole repair on driveways and parking lots
  • Patching failed sections of asphalt
  • Crack sealing to stop water getting under the base
  • Skin patches for surface raveling and shallow cracking, when the base is sound

Who it is for

This is for you if

  • A previous patch failed and you want the cause addressed this time
  • Your driveway has cracks or patches that keep coming back
  • Your pavement has cracks, potholes, or surface deterioration spreading
  • You are not sure whether you need a repair or a full replacement

How we do it

The process

  • AssessWe check whether the base is still sound or whether the failure goes deeper.
  • RemoveWe remove deteriorated material down to a stable base. We do not fill over it.
  • Tack and fillWe apply tack coat, then fill with hot or cold mix as the repair requires.
  • CompactWe compact thoroughly. This is not a fill-and-go patch.

Questions

Common questions

Why did my last patch fail?

Patches usually fail when the weak material underneath is left in place. We remove it to a stable base before we fill.

Do you use hot mix or cold mix?

We choose hot or cold mix based on the repair.

Do you patch before sealcoating?

Yes. Failed sections are repaired first, and sealcoating is applied only to sound asphalt.
