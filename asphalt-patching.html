What it covers

What we do

  • Pothole repair on driveways and parking lots
  • Patching failed sections of asphalt
  • Crack sealing to stop water getting under the base
  • Skin patches or thin overlays for surface raveling and shallow cracking, when the base is sound

Who it is for

This is for you if

  • A previous patch failed and you want the cause addressed this time
  • Your driveway has cracks or patches that keep coming back
  • Your pavement has potholes or surface deterioration that is spreading
  • You are not sure whether you need a repair or a full replacement

How we do it

How a patch is made to last

  1. 1. AssessWe check whether the base is still sound or whether the failure goes deeper.
  2. 2. RemoveWe remove the deteriorated material down to a stable base. We do not fill over it.
  3. 3. Tack and fillWe apply tack coat, then fill with hot or cold mix, depending on the repair.
  4. 4. CompactWe compact thoroughly. This is not a fill-and-go patch.

When a patch is not the right answer

If a short-term patch will not hold, we say so and explain what will protect the property better.

A failed base usually needs a larger repair or a replacement section. We will tell you which one applies.

Questions

Common questions

Why did my last patch fail?

Patches usually fail when the weak material underneath is left in place. We remove it to a stable base before we fill.

Is a skin patch the same as a full repair?

No. A skin patch or thin overlay suits surface raveling or shallow cracking when the base is sound. Deeper failures need a full repair.

Do you patch before sealcoating?

Yes. Failed sections are repaired first, and sealcoating goes only on sound asphalt.

Do you use hot mix or cold mix?

We choose hot or cold mix based on the repair.
